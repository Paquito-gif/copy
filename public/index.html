How it works

From fees to burns in three steps

Everything runs through one public wallet. Every copied trade, buyback and burn has its own transaction on Solana.

    FAQ

    Questions

    Who receives my coin's fees?

    The creator share, 5% of Float's fee, goes to the launchpad's public wallet. Your coin's holders keep their own share, paid by Float in the pair asset.

    What is the FOMO leaderboard?

    FOMO is a Solana trading app that ranks its traders by PnL. The engine copies the #1 wallet, with position-size and loss limits.

    Can the copy-trading wallet lose money?

    Yes. It always enters after the trader it copies, at a worse price. Limits cut losses, but no profit is guaranteed.

    Which coins get bought back?

    Every coin launched here, in proportion to the fees it generated. Bought-back coins are burned, and every burn shows up on the PnL page.

    Is this an official Float site?

    No. It's an independent interface that calls the Float program. The coins are real Float coins on Raydium LaunchLab.
